Secure Payments

Cloud PCI — Secure Payments

What is Cloud PCI? Enterprise-grade PCI DSS compliance for Contact Centres — without infrastructure change. Cloud PCI removes cardholder data entirely from your environment, supporting agent-assisted, automated, and hybrid payment experiences across every channel.
PCI DSS Compliant SAQ-A Eligible Platform Agnostic Omnichannel
DTMF Suppression Pause & Resume Pay by Link IVR Payments Voice, SMS, Chat, Bot PSP Agnostic De-scope Environment
⏸️

Pauseable

Automated pause-and-resume recording at the payment stage. Works with the agent desktop and extends via Pay by Link — letting customers complete transactions securely on their own device via SMS, webchat, or bot.

🔗

Pay by Link

Secure payment links delivered across any channel — SMS, email, bot, webchat. Agents can monitor progress in real time, giving customers full flexibility to pay on their preferred device.

🧑‍💼

Agent Pay

Comprehensive DTMF masking prevents card tones from entering your environment. Supports omnichannel interactions including speech recognition, digital chat, and automated voice flows.

📞

IVR Assist

Protect existing and new IVR payment journeys with a lightweight API integration. Card data routes directly to the secure platform — enabling SAQ-A eligibility and reduced scope without changing your IVR.
