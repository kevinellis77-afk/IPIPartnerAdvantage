SME Challenge

ElasticCX Engage: bridging the gap between UC and full CCaaS

Stop SME deal drop-off with a lower-friction bridge between UC and full ElasticCX.

Problem

The pricing cliff is where early deals stall

  • UC / PBX to full CCaaS feels like a 3–5x price jump
  • SME buyers with basic CX needs will not buy enterprise economics on day one
  • Deals fall back to UC-only before IPI gets traction
Engage smooths the commercial jump without diluting full ElasticCX.
Offer

Land earlier without discounting the platform

  • Voice + 1–2 digital channels with simple routing
  • Packaged onboarding keeps cost and delivery effort low
  • Clear upgrade path into full ElasticCX
Partner fit

Built to complement UC estates already in channel

  • Fits alongside Xelion, Gamma, Wildix and Voiceflex environments
  • Avoids a rip-and-replace conversation in SME
  • Gives UC-first partners a first step into CX
Bridge the gap, don’t discount the platform
Position Engage as enhanced telephony with light digital CX. It protects the value of full ElasticCX while giving partners a credible entry price point.
